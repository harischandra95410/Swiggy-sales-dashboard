
# 🍔 Swiggy Sales Dashboard Analysis using Advanced Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive **Swiggy Sales Dashboard** developed using **Advanced Excel**. The dashboard provides valuable insights into sales performance, customer satisfaction, order trends, and geographic distribution of revenue.

The objective of this project is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations and KPI tracking.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

To analyze Swiggy's food delivery sales performance and customer behavior using key business metrics and visual dashboards.

The dashboard helps answer important business questions such as:

* How are sales performing over time?
* Which cities generate the highest revenue?
* What is the contribution of Veg vs Non-Veg food sales?
* How do customer ratings impact business performance?
* Which states contribute the most revenue?

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI                     | Description                                    |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Total Sales (₹)         | Overall revenue generated from food orders     |
| Total Orders            | Total number of food orders received           |
| Average Order Value (₹) | Average revenue generated per order            |
| Average Rating          | Customer satisfaction level across restaurants |
| Rating Count            | Total number of customer reviews received      |

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Monthly Sales Trend

**Objective:** Analyze month-wise revenue performance.

**Visualization:** Line Chart

---

### 2. Daily Sales Trend

**Objective:** Monitor sales and order variations across different days of the week.

**Visualization:** Column Chart

---

### 3. Total Sales by Food Type (Veg vs Non-Veg)

**Objective:** Compare revenue contribution between Veg and Non-Veg food categories.

**Visualization:** Pie/Donut Chart

---

### 4. State-wise Sales Analysis

**Objective:** Identify revenue distribution across different states.

**Visualization:** Map Chart

---

### 5. Quarterly Performance Summary

**Objective:** Compare sales, ratings, and order volume across quarters.

**Visualization:** KPI Cards & Summary Charts

---

### 6. Top 5 Cities by Sales

**Objective:** Identify cities contributing the highest revenue.

**Visualization:** Bar Chart

---

### 7. Weekly Trend Analysis

**Objective:** Track weekly fluctuations in sales and identify peak business periods.

**Visualization:** Trend Chart
